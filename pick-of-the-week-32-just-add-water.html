Pick of the Week

Just Add Water

06.01.2016/By Adam Fricke/The Road

Photo · Slot canyon waterfall in Utah

It's no secret that I'm happiest in the water. It sounds like hippie-talk coming from a Floridian surf nut, but it's true. When I'm away from water for a long period of time, I get cranky.

Last time we were in Utah (Moab to be exact), I was in a bit of a funk. It was around that time that I realized that to get out of said funk, I just need to add water. That being said, this week as we left the California coast to head back into the Utah desert, I knew I was heading into the wrong state of mind.

But wouldn't you know that surfing or not, any kind of water alleviates my bad moods. We went for a lesser known hike to this slot canyon waterfall and I couldn't have been happier. Crossing the stream multiple times throughout the hike to get to this amazing little spot was all the water necessary to keep me in good spirits, even in the desert.
