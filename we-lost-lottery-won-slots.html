We Lost the Lottery But Won the Slots

06.02.2016/By Justin Fricke/The Road

After we lost the walk-in lottery for The Wave, Adam and I were feeling kind of bummed. Kanab's a tiny little desert town without much going on as we found ourselves trying to find something to do to pass the time. After a morning of work we went to Kanarraville Trail Head about an hour or two from Kanab. My friend George had told Adam and I about this hike that'd take us to a picturesque destination.

As we pulled into the parking lot neither of us were too stoked. We just left the Sierra Nevada Mountains and weren't too happy about being back in the desert. The desert's a beautiful place, but all the red rocks start looking the same.

Walking up the manmade dirt stairs I heard the faint sound of water flowing downstream and remembered we hadn't seen this part of the desert. We were hiking towards a slot canyon.

The air was warm and dry as we crossed the same cool river countless times. Adam tried his hardest not to get his shoes wet, but that went out the window at the third crossing when there was no rock bridge to cross.

An hour later we came to the opening of a slot canyon, the first we've ever seen. Adam took his time and the adrenaline pumping through my veins made me stupidly hurry through. It got cold real quick and I was stopped dead in my tracks when we came to the waterfall we were chasing. With the spring snow melt in full force the water was cascading over the 12 foot falls making for the perfect scene.

As we were setting up our shots a huge smile overcame my face. I was reminded that this is my job and I have the best job in the world. Sure I may not always be stoked on the area, but there's always a silver lining and this silver lining is seeing something I've never seen before.

I climbed the ladder up to the top of the falls as the light started to turn pristine. Adam followed suit and if it weren't for the setting sun, we would've stayed in that slot canyon for hours.

Hiking towards the setting sun Adam jumped and clicked his heels together. Feeling high on life we were ready to get some sleep and try our luck at The Wave lottery again the next morning.
