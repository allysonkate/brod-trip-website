Midwestern Hospitality

By Adam Fricke • October 4, 2016

Being from Florida, we’re no strangers to southern hospitality. But never having spent much time in the Midwest, I had no idea that Midwestern hospitality is trying to give the south a run for its money!

Original Photo Lost in the Archive

160926_Nebraska-005.jpg

From // Midwestern Hospitality

We didn’t think there was much to do in Nebraska. Luckily my friend from high school recently moved to Omaha and invited us to stay with he and his wife and their adorable son. We ended up “having” to spend an extra day with the Gray family. Maybe it was mountain biking down the neighborhood hill in the dark wearing flip-flops that made us believe Nebraska wasn’t boring. Or maybe it was the homemade fajitas, talks about what we’ve accomplished and left undone since high school, or baby Jackson’s ukulele serenade that made us spend more time. Whatever it was, we’re glad we stayed.

We planned on blowing right through Kansas too. But when we caught word that our Aunt Sharon, whom we’d never met, lives in Topeka and was willing to show us around, we figured we couldn’t pass that up. Lo and behold, we had so much fun hanging out with her, her amazingly well-behaved pups, and her hilarious family that we ended up staying an extra day in Kansas too!

Original Photo Lost in the Archive

160929_Kansas_AuntSharon-019.jpg

From // Midwestern Hospitality

Our Aunt Sharon is not only a dog trainer, active book club member of 22 years, and magnificent meatloaf chef, but she also used to do a good amount of climbing in her 40s and 50s. Needless to say, she and Justin had quite a bit to talk about. Although she doesn’t climb anymore, she assures us that once she gets a new knee, she’ll be “hell on wheels!” And I believe her! I was surprised to be sad to leave Kansas, but family has the tendency to make me feel that way.

After a quick Gateway Arch visit in Missouri, we kept truckin’ to Springfield, Illinois. Springfield is famous for being Abraham Lincoln’s hometown. But in the Fricke family it’s famous for being the hometown of our Aunt Wilma. Aunt Wilma recently turned 90 and celebrated in Florida with our entire extended family in June. Since we weren’t around for that, we made sure we got our butts to Springfield to spend time together. She is the most active 90-year-old you’ll ever meet. She was even eager to drive us around Springfield to give us the grand tour, show us where our dad lived growing up, and of course make a visit to Pease’s Candy Shop, a Springfield staple that claims it’s “local since Lincoln.”

As if we didn’t feel at home in the Midwest already, our friend Ashley invited us to stay with her and her boyfriend, Michael, in Chicago. After a full tour of the city, the best cinnamon rolls I’ve had in my entire life, and the chance to play any guitar I could imagine at Chicago Music Exchange, we were treated to a night on the town and a great night’s sleep on an air mattress. I woke up in their beautiful apartment with a view of the city and a delicious plate of turkey bacon, eggs, and pancakes. Now I’m quite convinced that the Midwest is home to some of the nicest people in the country!
