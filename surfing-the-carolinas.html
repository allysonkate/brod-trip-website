Surfing the Carolinas

For as long as I can remember, my first surf session of each new year has been at New Smyrna Beach, Florida. It usually occurs within the first few days of the year and consists of small, choppy, longboard waves. I’m usually wearing a wetsuit although the water isn’t that cold.

This year was different. It was still just a few days into the new year. But it was at a little pier in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. And it was ball-chilling cold. I’ve never seen Myrtle Beach as a surf destination, as few rarely do. Even the guys we met in the water couldn’t figure out why the hell we would leave Florida to come surf a modest pier in South Carolina. Nevertheless, they were super cool dudes and gave us some intel about their little SC surf scene in between college football banter.

We also used that pier as an opportunity for our first shower of the trip. It was breathtaking, but more in the literal sense, as the air was in the 40’s and it was dark with a steady windchill.

After getting a taste of fun waves, we had to have more. We took our chances and drove all the way up to Cedar Island, NC to catch a 7am ferry the following morning. We loaded the van onto the ferry and set sail to the Outer Banks. On the ferry, we utilized the passenger lounge and met 3 cool dudes from Wrightsville, NC. We had almost 3 hours to kill on the ferry so we talked about everything from surfing to French films.

When we arrived in the Outer Banks, it was like we were on a different planet. It was overcast and drizzly without a soul in sight. We stopped to look for waves almost every chance we got. To our dismay, the wind and swell were never quite working in our favor. But we took the opportunity to explore each area and take pictures. We even spent the better part of an afternoon in Kitty Hawk, visiting the Wright Brothers Memorial.

After loitering around the chain of islands for a few days, we finally got a sunny day with offshore winds. The water was a freezing 51 degrees with wind gusts up to 30mph. This time of year is not ideal for surf at OBX but I had to try while I was there. Justin didn’t paddle out and I didn’t blame him. He hung out on the beach to film me as I surfed but there wasn’t much to film.

I surfed 3 sessions at Jeanette’s Pier and got a total of 3 waves. An extremely cold, disappointing afternoon in the water. But it left me wanting more. We will be back to conquer OBX later in the year when the swell is good. I want redemption.
