Free Beach House in Santa Barbara!

04.12.2016byJustin Fricke//Leave a Comment

With spirits high after a few days in Death Valley, we were on the road again headed west. After a morning hike with Corinne at Mosaic Canyon we took an alternate route to the coast that she recommended. We’d be adding a little time to our already four-hour drive, but we’d skip a nasty stretch of washboard road AND get to see some amazing views of the Sierra Nevada Mountains. The views did not disappoint.

Four or five hours later and we were rolling up to a sleepy little beach community outside of Santa Barbara. Our friends Megan and Michael (Fresh Off The Grid) invited us to hang out with them for a few days at a beach house. What would you do? We said, “yes!”

Before we officially set out on The Bro’d Trip we got an email from Fresh Off The Grid asking if we’d be interested in hanging out at some point this year should our paths cross. For four months we’ve been trying to find a place to meet up and the stars finally aligned! We offered up smelly vanlife hugs and for once we didn’t feel the need to explain or apologize for our rank smell. They get why we smelled like three-day-old sweat with a seasoning of desert dust atop our clumpy hair. We’re them and they’re us, sorta.

Instead of living in a Sprinter Van, they’re living the Sedan Life and run a camp cooking blog. The four of us collectively understand what each other are doing with our lives, meaning there’s no need to justify our life’s decisions. We bounced ideas off each other, shared creative vibes, and bitched about the trials and tribulations of living on the road over some cheap beer.

That night the four of us hung out over drinks and a new dish they learned about on their trip to Cuba. They told us stories from their trip they were working on for their blog. We took to our role of taste testing and realized that we were in the presence of camp cooking royalty. Their dishes blew our flavored rice and canned chicken out of the water preserve that our pre-cooked chicken sits in!

Over the next couple days we all got to know each other better. Adam and I grabbed a few rights at the point break just out in front of the beach house and we all were able to get some much needed work done in between planning our next moves. Before The Bro’d Trip began I hoped we’d meet similar minded people to share life on the road with. In Texas I got a taste of what that’d be like, but our friends had to get back to their lives. But this time, the four of us started planning our next moves and my hopes were turning into our reality.

The original plan was for the four of us to backpack Sespe Hot Springs Tuesday through Friday, but the weather had other plans. Knowing none of us wanted to go backpacking in the rain and subject our camera gear to potential destruction, and knowing the area’s prone to flash floods, we looked for new options. Instead the four of us set our sights north towards Big Sur.
