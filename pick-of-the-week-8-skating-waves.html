Pic(k) of the Week — #8

Skating Waves

12.16.2015byJustin Fricke//Leave a Comment

Original photo unavailable — lost in the archive

Expected file: 151213_Surf-2639-1.jpg

Every surfer has their own style. I like to cruise, Adam makes these effortless carving turns, some surfers like the snap turns that throw a ton of spray, and others like to catch air. Our friend Ricky likes to jumble up a lot of everything into his surfing. The kid's really fun to watch. It's kind of like watching someone skating on waves. Dropping in he picks up speed, pumps through different sections of the wave to gain more speed and then goes flying in the air. Most of the time his feet leave the board after he launches into the air and only occasionally lands an air in a contest. Whether he actually lands the air or not, he's always laughing and joking as he paddles back out to the lineup to catch some more waves.

In a way his surfing is kind of like a box of chocolates, you just don't know what you're going to get. When you take his picture, you know you're going to walk away with a plethora of images of him tearing up the wave and flying in the air.
