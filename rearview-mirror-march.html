Rearview Mirror: March

04.14.2016byAdam Fricke//4 Comments

Rearview Mirror is a 12-part series. Each of the 12 posts represents one month of life on the road, an ode to the last 31 days. Buckle up and hang on to those armrests with all you’ve got, we’re going fast.

—

Well, we’re 1/4 into our year on the road. It’s flown right by. So let’s take a look at the month that gave us a run for our money, quite literally. With 2 separate mechanical issues for Jolene, March was determined to break us. But we’re well prepared and determined to keep this dream alive. We’ve grown to love our nomadic ways and refuse to give in. Despite the troubles, the great times have trumped all those tough days. And if I’ve learned anything over these last 3 months, it’s that the best is yet to come.

Here are a handful of the highlights: We found yet another weird place in Texas. Border Patrol gave us a thorough pat-down. Justin completed his Birthday Challenge! Adam embraced his inner tourist while climbing Hueco Tanks. We witnessed a legend in the making in El Paso. Working on the road isn’t easy, but man is it fulfilling. Friends are getting harder and harder to say goodbye to. White Sands kicked our asses, but we did do some sand sledding! Jolene needed some new parts which weren’t cheap. Adam won us some breakfast money at the Roulette table! Easter was spent with good friends over a game of Spikeball. The Internet introduced us to yet another awesome friend. Justin surfed in California for the very first time.

—

Thanks for reading our words, watching our vlogs, and looking at our pictures. Thanks for being a part of this journey.
