VLOG_48 | BTS AT MERRELL HQ!

By Justin Fricke • October 17, 2016
Watch on YouTube →

Have you ever wondered what goes on behind the scenes at Merrell HQ in Rockford, MI? Or Maybe you’ve wondered if people from the Merrell team actually get outside and enjoy the outdoors. They do work for an outdoor brand, after all. Or maybe you thought robots ran the daily operations at Merrell HQ.

Rest assured, there are real life people who make the magic, shoe and apparel magic, happen at Merrell and they totally live the brand. While we were in The Mitten we hung out with our friends at Merrell and they showed us a great time mountain biking, fishing, disc golfing, and a few more shenanigans; after we got the behind the scenes tour.
