Rearview Mirror: September

By Adam Fricke • October 6, 2016

Rearview Mirror is a 12-part series. Each of the 12 posts represents one month of life on the road, an ode to the last 30 days. Buckle up and hang on to those armrests with all you’ve got, we’re going fast.

—

SEPTEMBER

It doesn’t take a rocket scientist to figure out that we’re a bit “behind schedule” when it comes to the number of states we’ve visited so far this year. September was dedicated to covering a ton of ground. With 10 new states under our belts, our count is up to 30. We’re back on track to hitting our 50-state goal!

160921_NorthDakota_MountainBiking-015.jpg

—

HERE’S THE NUMERICAL BREAKDOWN OF OUR NINTH MONTH ON THE ROAD:

Showers Taken: 12

Every 3 days isn’t so bad, right?

Meals Eaten Out: 13

Home cooked meals and local favorites!

Nights in a Real Bed: 8

Shout out to friends, family, and strangers for you your beds, couches, and floors!

Miles Driven: 4,689

The road is home.

States Visited: MT, WY, SD, ND, MN, IA, NE, KS, MO, IL

The most states we’ve covered in a month! Coming to a town near you.

Photos Taken: 4,198

Justin: 2,521 | Adam: 1,677

 

HERE ARE A HANDFUL OF THE HIGHLIGHTS:

  • We went from Florida to Montana.
  • Three national parks were our homes for a week!
  • Adam worked on his patience.
  • Mt. Rushmore was made to be climbed and alpaca are meant to be chased.
  • The Badlands were actually really good.
  • We met Switchfoot in Minnesota!
  • The Enchanted Highway was magical.
  • We’re back east of the Mississippi!

—

Thanks for reading our words, watching our vlogs, and looking at our pictures. Thanks for being a part of this journey.
