Covered in Saw Dust

12.02.2015byJustin Fricke//Leave a Comment

Original photo lost in the archive

Expected file: 151129_Saw-1516-1.jpg

We've said it in virtually every post that we're a couple of idiots when it comes to using power tools. We haven't been exposed to them much, but we have a general understanding of how to use each tool we've needed, or so I thought. While we were getting ready to cut and install our furring strips, Adam asked me if he could take a shot at cutting all the furring strips to size with the circular saw. I had been doing most of the cutting up to this point and without hesitation I said, "of course!" Little did I know that this was his first time using a circular saw.

I gave him a quick crash course on how to release the safety and how to line it up with the line that he had already traced. He was off to the races with the safety glasses and ear protection on and circular saw buzzing forward. That left me to take video and get some images of him using the circular saw for the first time while getting covered in saw dust.
