Pick of the Week

Be Careful Of Your Knee!

04.20.2016/By Justin Fricke/The Road

Photo · Adam Fricke rock hopping at Red Rock Canyon

If you watch any of our vlogs, you'll see us scampering over rocks, hiking all over the place, climbing, surfing, and doing whatever else we please. If we make it look easy, we're doing our job and you'll probably be surprised to hear that Adam obliterated his right knee in high school.

He was playing a pick up game of basketball with some friends and when he jumped he heard a loud pop. After returning to earth rather ungracefully, he called it quits for the day, hobbled back to his car, and drove home. A few days later he was being told by the doctor that he annihilated his right ACL. It required the surgeon to take part of his hamstring and weave it into a new ACL. To top it all off, he found that Adam's meniscus was torn and folded in thirds. It folded in half and then folded again! A long recovery over the summer leading into his senior year of high school and his knee was healed.

Ask him how his knee is doing these days and he'll tell you it's alright. Watch him hop over rocks or surf any wave and it looks like nothing ever happened. But look closely and you'll see him being very meticulous as to where he's placing his feet on uneven terrain, as is the case with this photo.

Before hopping off this rock he stood there for a minute to check out his landing. Once I was set with the camera he took a graceful leap and it turned into an ungraceful landing, so he could protect his knee.

No knee was harmed in the taking of this photo.
