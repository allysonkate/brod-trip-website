Vlog_35 | Ice Caves in Washington!

July 11, 2016  •  Justin Fricke
Watch on YouTube →

While Florida (where we grew up) was sweltering in the humid summer heat on the 4th of July, we were wearing jackets in Olympic National Park. It’s still chilly up here along the coast of the Pacific Northwest! Aside from that, a lot of the Pacific Northwest is reminding us of Florida. Greenery, kind people, and terrific beer make it feel like we’re back in Florida.

Except for the ice caves. You won’t find any of those back home!
