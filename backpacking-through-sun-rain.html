BACKPACKING THROUGH SUN & RAIN

08.11.2016 • Justin Fricke
⚠ Partial Post — Only a partial excerpt survived in the Wayback Machine archive.

Driving through the Mt. Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest, my friend Heidi and I saw every campsite filled with campers. These weren’t fair weather campers, like myself, these campers live in the Pacific Northwest. It rains often in the rain forest and these people want to get outside. At least that’s what Heidi told me as we drove looking for a spot.
