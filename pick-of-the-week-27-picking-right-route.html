Picking the Right Route

From // Picking the Right Route

In climbing, picking the right route is crucial. Like most crucial decisions, it's not easy to choose. You have to know your skill level, what types of climbs you're good at (long, short, crimpy, slopey…) and only if you pick one in your wheelhouse, do you have a shot at completing it.

Justin has gotten pretty solid at picking routes that are in his range. He's lanky so routes with holds far apart are usually a breeze for him. Then if I try to do the same one, I have a hard time on it.

The same can be said for any aspect of life. Any "route" is possible, but it's best to take into consideration your strengths, weaknesses, and what that route has to offer. Oh, and don't be afraid to consult the guidebook!
