Working With the Merrell Team at HQ

10.20.2016 • Justin Fricke

I think it’s easy to think of a brand, or define a brand, by its logo, slogan, and products. What I think we, as consumers, really miss is that there’s more to a brand than its logo, slogan, and products. We forget about the people that live the brand and make it what it is. I often times forget about this, but was reminded of it when we got to spend the better part of the week with various people from the Merrell team when we were in The Mitten of Michigan.

Before our trip even began we were told that we’d have an incredible time while we were in Michigan and that the Merrell team would make sure of it. Not totally knowing what to expect, we walked into Merrell HQ and once we got past security (sounds way more intense than it was), we met up with our friends that we’ve met between the Tough Mudder in Arizona, a photo shoot in Puerto Rico, and a happy hour in Seattle. We didn’t have to be at HQ until 11:30am, but we wanted to get there at 10:00am because even though we have certain obligations to fulfill, everyone we’ve met at Merrell has become our friends. And we wanted a little bit of time to see and hang out with our friends.

One thing I didn’t expect was for so many people at Merrell to be following our trip. While we were hanging out at HQ other members of the Merrell team (finance, development, design, etc.) would walk by and occasionally recognize us and we’d get the chance to hang out and make new friends. On top of that when we’d meet and hang out with other people from the Merrell team they’d always talk about their co workers. Always in a positive light and it was so much fun to get to finally put faces to names.

Two things I wasn’t totally expecting:

1: Everyone I’ve met at Merrell lives the brand.

2: Everyone loves what they do.

At times I’ve wondered if the people who work at other outdoor brands actually live the brand. I think we all wonder if the people making the big decisions actually get out on the trails, live an active lifestyle, and use the products they sell. Everyone that I’ve met at Merrell does, 100%. It didn’t matter if they had a meeting early in the morning or later in the day, they carved time out of their schedule to take us mountain biking, fishing, and disc golfing. And it seemed to be completely normal for someone to show up a little sweaty and/or reeking of fish.

On our last day there Adam asked a couple people if they ever have to take their work home. Although Merrell highly encourages everyone to keep their work at work, we noticed that at times people take their work home with them without complaints. How many people can actually say that they don’t mind taking some work home from time to time? I’d put my money on not too many, but I think that really goes to show that a brand (or any company for that matter) is only as good as its people.

Never in my wildest dreams did I ever think that I’d get to work with a company like Merrell and I know Adam feels the same. And while I’ve loved the Merrell brand for years, actually working with and getting to know the people that make this brand so great is more rewarding than having that “sponsored” stamp on a resume or website because the brand is only as good as its people. And the team I’ve met at Merrell is what most companies dream about.
