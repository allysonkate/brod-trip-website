Pic(k) of the Week #36 | The Life of Luxury

June 29, 2016  •  Adam Fricke

It's no secret that Visit Idaho hooked us up with some amazing experiences during our stay. In addition to setting us up with some fun outdoorsy activities to do, they also got us into a couple pretty cool local hotels. We were living the life of luxury. The Modern even gave us a gift card to their restaurant which we had no trouble putting to good use.

Special treatment aside, we really appreciate the things that we took for granted and don't have anymore. Like showers, a couch, microwave, and especially comfy beds to flop onto!

Pic Info

  • f/3.2
  • 1/320 sec
  • ISO 1600
  • Shot RAW
