Rearview Mirror: The Bro’d Trip 2016

January 10, 2017  •  Adam Fricke

2016 was one heck of a year for us. We accomplished a goal we’d taken 2 years to dream up and plan out. Here’s a numerical recap of what our unforgettable year on the road looked like.

366Days on the RoadOne full year living on the road. The fact that it was a leap year was just an added bonus!
261Nights in the VanShout out to friends, family, and strangers for your beds, couches, and floors on those other 105 days!
146Showers TakenGoing a week without a shower was common — often times access came in bursts of several days in a row.
244Meals Eaten OutEvery meal not cooked ourselves — fast food, local delicacies, and home-cooked meals with friends.
50Places VisitedU.S. States, Washington D.C., Puerto Rico, 3 Canadian Provinces (British Columbia, Alberta, and Yukon Territory)
43,918Miles DrivenThe road is home.
$20,885Dollars SpentFood, gas, van insurance, maintenance, parking, postcards & postage, and misc. fees. Does not include van purchase or build-out.
43,162Photos TakenJustin: 23,096  |  Adam: 20,066

Thank you for reading our words, watching our vlogs, and looking at our pictures. Thank you for being a part of this journey.
