UNCHARTED TERRITORY | PUERTO RICO

08.30.2016 • Adam Fricke

In the planning stages of The Bro’d Trip, we entertained the idea of visiting Puerto Rico but decided that it wasn’t really a viable option. Mainly because we wouldn’t be able to afford the airfare to get there. We were a little bummed but weren’t devastated because after all, it’s a U.S. territory, not one of the 50 states.

But while we were in Idaho, we got an interesting email from our friends at Merrell, asking us if we’d be interested in exploring some uncharted territory: modeling. No, we’re not models. Far from it. But Merrell likes to use their ambassadors as much as possible when launching new products and thought that we, and the founders of Women Who Hike, would make a great team for showing off a line of their 2017 gear.

So what do you say to a company that wants to fly you to Puerto Rico for 3 days of exploring, sleeping in hotels, and cervezas? We said YES!

We ditched the van in Mississippi and flew out of New Orleans. It was the first time we’d been in a plane since leaving home and in a hotel since March. We were whisked away to an introductory dinner with the crew and given a rundown of the week ahead. One aspect of the shoot that got me slightly nervous was learning about the wardrobe aspect of the shoot. They would have someone lay out different outfits for us and we’d have to put them on and ask ourselves how “that first shirt” looked again. We spent the better part of 5 hours pulling “looks” together for the entire 3-day shoot. This gave us plenty of time to get to know the crew, photographer, videographer, and of course the Women Who Hike.

We’ve been in touch with Nicole and Savanah via the interwebs for the past few months. When we passed through their home states of California and Oregon we made attempts to hang out with them but couldn’t quite make our schedules intersect. So when we found out they’d be our counterparts as we explored Puerto Rico, we couldn’t have been more excited. Women Who Hike brings women of all backgrounds together through the common interest of hiking. The camaraderie they generate via their social media posts brings people from their phone screens to the trails as they make lasting connections centered around facing challenges together in the outdoors.

Our first day of shooting was spent in El Yunque National Forest. We woke up before the sun, packed our gear, and drove to the base of the mountain. With a backdrop of thick tropical forest, we hiked and explored while our crew captured everything beautifully. What stood out to me about the crew was their work ethic throughout the entire week. They were up before everyone and were the last ones to stop working each day. And all the while, they were carrying heavy equipment and instructing us in order to get the shots they envisioned.

Thankfully, this was a lifestyle shoot which meant that instead of trying to pose a certain way, we were doing things that came naturally to us. This included hiking, exploring the old city of San Juan, riding bikes, cracking jokes, and even sipping rum drinks from coconuts. The more time we spent together, the more our crew felt like a little family. Standing in front of the camera would’ve felt a bit uncomfortable for me but being surrounded by good company gave me the ability to goof around and be myself.

My favorite day of shooting was the final day. We hiked through the rainforest to a waterfall hidden in the trees. Amongst those trees were rope swings and a handful of rocks that were begging to be jumped from. Needless to say we didn’t have to be coaxed into jumping, swinging, flipping, and having a blast.

The final shoot of the trip was probably the toughest. Not because it was a long hike (it was on the beach just out the front doors of the hotel!) but because of the wardrobe. Justin and Savanah were to be showing off a couple long-sleeved additions to Merrell’s apparel collection as we have a campfire on the beach. The only problem was that it was about 90 degrees out with 100% humidity. While the scene looks like it was nice and cool out, we were all miserably hot! We made the best of it, goofed around, had fun, and got all the shots we needed. And just like that, it was a wrap! After Andrew snapped a family photo, the only logical thing to do next was to go out to a local restaurant for some ceviche, arepas, and cervezas to celebrate a job well done!
