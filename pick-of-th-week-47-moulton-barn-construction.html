Pic(k) of th Week #47 | Moulton Barn Construction

By Justin Fricke • September 14, 2016

Before we visited Grand Teton National Park, Adam told me about a barn he wanted to visit. Neither of us were too sure what the draw to the barn is, aside from the fact that it looks incredible with the Tetons in the background.

I slid out of bed early and into the cold Wyoming morning, grabbed my stuff, and headed over to take some photos of the barn at sunrise with a slew of others. We all stood there clicking the button on our shutter release remotes, “getting the shot.” A tour bus pulled up and I cracked a smile and motioned for Adam to take a look.

A small group came walking over from the bus and their young guide joined them once he had parked. They all gawked at this barn and the tour guide started spitting some history. One little bit stuck with me, that this barn took 26 years to build! All the wood was harvested from a location 7 miles away and the family had to harvest the wood by hand and transport it back to the homestead with their livestock on top of doing things to stay alive like hunting game and harvesting crops.

That’s what’s so special about this barn, I guess.
