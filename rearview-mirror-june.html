Rearview Mirror: June

July 21, 2016  •  Adam Fricke

Rearview Mirror is a 12-part series. Each of the 12 posts represents one month of life on the road, an ode to the last 30 days. Buckle up and hang on to those armrests with all you’ve got, we’re going fast.

June was an exciting month for us. It marks our halfway point and brought us back together with Jolene. Sure, there were plenty more ups and downs, but we’re blowing through obstacles like no other. We’re a little “behind schedule” in terms of states visited, but we’re certain that the next 6 months will be just as exciting as the last. We’re on our way!

The Numbers

Showers Taken: 6 — Few and far between.

Meals Eaten Out: 12 — Fast food and slow turns. We’re ballin’ on a budget.

Highlights

  • The Narrows in Zion were… narrow.
  • Our friends Beth and Sprocket gave us a peek at the life of a peak bagger.
  • Adam surfed in Colorado and it didn’t go too well….
  • We visited 4 states at once and got Jolene back!
  • Solitude was found at the Grand Canyon.
  • Boulder, CO welcomed us into summer.
  • We had to work a long weekend in Breckenridge and it was AWESOME.
  • Visit Idaho showed us a hell of a good time in The Gem State.

Thanks for reading our words, watching our vlogs, and looking at our pictures. Thanks for being a part of this journey.
