Van Troubles in New Mexico

03.25.2016byJustin Fricke//Leave a Comment

What would a road trip be without a little car trouble? Or in our case, van trouble. After a long, sleepless night out at White Sands National Monument, we came back to a solid punch in the gnads. I cranked the engine and Jolene’s “Check Engine” light showed up. I knew that there was a great chance she needed a new fuel filter. Lucky for us, we had one handy and I know how to change a fuel filter! But that would’ve been too easy. After an hour under the hood at the White Sands Visitor Center parking lot, her check engine light still shown bright as day.

Situations like this usually make or break a road trip. In this case, it made ours. We take great care of Jolene, get her serviced when service is due, and we do our best to avoid all the pot holes waiting to eat up her wheels. But we know that preventative maintenance and care only goes so far. Some sort of a blowout was bound to occur and that blowout was her EGR Valve.

We took her up to Albuquerque to get a brand new EGR Valve installed. We could’ve gone the route of having a mechanic clean out the old one, but that would’ve cost twice as much and if we can get a new part at half the cost, why not?

Lesson 1: Do Your Research

The service at the first dealership we went to was subpar. It took them 5 hours just to run a diagnostic and we didn’t hear from anyone until they were closing. That’s when we learned they wouldn’t even have the necessary parts until two days later. That’s when I made a couple calls and found another dealership, four hours away, willing to rush the part to their location and get us back on the road in no time.

Lesson 2: Pinch Your Pennies

This has been the first real gut check on our trip, and it’s good it came now than further down the road or in the middle of nowhere. Through 12 states and 2.5 months, we’ve lived below our means, and that’s paid off. Since we’ve sold some shirts, saved our pennies, and picked up some freelance work on the side, we didn’t have to dip into our “Oh Sh!t” fund.

With that whole ordeal behind us, we set our sights on Arizona for a new journey and a fresh new start.
